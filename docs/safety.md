# Rover safety

These rules apply to everyone who works on or near the rover. The rover is heavy, and its motors can move without warning.

## Before the motors move

Make sure that all of these are true:

1. A person has the E-stop within reach.
2. The rover is on blocks with its wheels off the ground, or nobody and nothing is in the test area.
3. Everyone nearby knows that the motors are about to move.
4. Everyone agrees how long the run lasts.

## During a run

- If anything unexpected happens, press the E-stop.
- Keep your hands away from the wheels and the excavator.
- Don't disable or lengthen the rover's command timeout.
- When the run ends, send a zero command.

## Flashing

Flash the Jetson or the Teensy only when a lead tells you to and is present.

## Batteries

Follow the battery rules in [rules.md](rules.md#batteries). The rules apply in the lab and at the competition.

## If something goes wrong

1. Press the E-stop.
2. If there's fire or smoke:
    1. Leave the area.
    2. Set off the fire alarm.
    3. From a safe place, call 999.
3. Tell a lead what happened, even if nobody was hurt.
