# Rover safety

These rules apply to everyone who works on or near the rover.

## Before you command a motor

Check that:

1. A person has the E-stop within reach.
2. The rover is on blocks with its wheels off the ground. Or nobody and nothing is in the test area.
3. Everyone nearby knows the motors are about to move.
4. Everyone agrees how long the run lasts. If nobody sets a duration, use 5 seconds.

## During a run

- If anything unexpected happens, press the E-stop.
- Keep your hands away from the wheels and the excavator.
- Send commands faster than the rover-side timeout. Never send one command and leave it running.
- Do not disable or lengthen the rover-side timeout.
- When the run ends, send a zero command.

## Flashing

Take precautions when you flash the Jetson or the Teensy.

## If something goes wrong

1. Press the E-stop.
2. If there is fire or smoke, do these steps in order:
   a. Leave the area.
   b. Set off the fire alarm.
   c. From a safe place, call 999.
3. Tell a lead what happened, even if nobody was hurt.
